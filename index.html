Services

Press releases and media outreach

An event, a new hire, a service expansion or a fundraiser is an opportunity to seek coverage with a professionally written press release. The service includes a targeted list of local and trade reporters, personal pitch emails and a report on the coverage that follows. The client approves one document and I handle the list, pitches and follow-up.

Learn more

Ghostwriting for attorneys

Blog posts, client alerts and bylined articles with insights and guidance from the perspective of the attorney, written from a short interview and reviewed by the attorney before publishing. Each piece is customized to the voice of the firm and the attorney. The content draws from my experience writing legal content since 2021.

Learn more

Ghostwriting for real estate professionals

Market updates, newsletters, agent bios, LinkedIn posts and area guides built on the insights and knowledge of the agent, leveraging the experience of a writer who has worked in real estate since 2005 in sales, marketing, administration and copywriting. Clients and prospects hear from the agent on a regular schedule, even in the busiest months.

Learn more

Conference to Content

The effort and planning that go into a conference are significant, so why should the content evaporate when the conference ends? I attend the client event, panel or conference in person, record the sessions for accuracy and write the articles, posts and summary pieces. A single conference can supply months of articles and posts.

Learn more

Architectural property narratives and listing descriptions

Architectural narratives, listing descriptions, property stories and community lifestyle pages for neighborhoods and developments. The story behind a home is often what earns coverage from media outlets, and a written narrative that honors the architecture keeps the agent’s name tied to the property long after it sells.

Newsletter distribution and management

A newsletter is a cost-effective means of connecting with customers. This service covers the writing, formatting, scheduling and distribution of newsletters on a regular cadence, with list management and basic reporting, on the platform the client prefers, including Mailchimp, Constant Contact and Beehiiv.

Website creation and management

Focused, straightforward websites for small businesses and professionals: a few clearly written pages that load quickly, explain the business and make contact easy. The writing is included, and each site can carry a news section that collects the press releases and articles produced together.

About Copacetic Marketing

My name is Fritts Causby, and I am the writer behind Copacetic Marketing. Copacetic Marketing is a one-writer firm today, and I bring in designers, writers and editors when it is necessary.

Since 2008, I have written press releases and marketing copy for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties, supporting thousands of agents across Southern and Central California. I have also worked as a ghostwriter for Ward and Smith, P.A., a regional law firm with offices across the Carolinas, since 2021.

My work has appeared in Inman Real Estate News and Wrightsville Beach Magazine, and my pitches have led to coverage in Robb Report and Mansion Global. Press releases I have created for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties have been distributed without my byline to numerous local and regional publications. I also wrote sponsored content for the Wilmington Business Journal for eight years, and I am a freelancer for Wrightsville Beach Magazine.

How it works

Each engagement begins with a brief call or an exchange of emails, whichever suits the client's schedule. A clearly written proposal defines the scope and the price of the project. I prepare a draft for review and revisions, and the finished work is published or distributed. Most projects take 2 to 4 business days, depending on complexity. Rush delivery within 24 to 48 hours is available for an additional fee.
