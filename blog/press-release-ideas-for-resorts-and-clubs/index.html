Press Release Ideas for Resorts and Clubs

Fritts Causby  ·  Copacetic Marketing  ·  2 min read

Resorts and clubs generate more news than most businesses; however, the majority of that news is never published. There are numerous events that can earn coverage, such as renovations and reopenings. Readers enjoy seeing the before-and-after details, the name of the designer and the date that guests can return.

A new chef, a seasonal menu, a wine dinner or a partnership with a local farm could become a feature for a culinary section in a local publication.

Golf and tennis tournaments, regattas, holiday programs and concerts could be announced ahead of time to increase attendance and reported on after the event is over to increase engagement.

Travel awards, golf course rankings and industry honors can be promoted to raise awareness. The name of each honor should be stated exactly as the awarding body gives it.

Additional news-worthy events could include a fundraiser that was hosted at the property, scholarships and partnerships with local schools. Even the hiring of a new general manager, a golf professional or dockmaster can be an item of interest to the local business community.

Each release should include high-resolution photos, a quote from management and, where a price appears, the full price including mandatory fees. Copacetic Marketing writes the copy, builds the media list and handles the follow-up.
