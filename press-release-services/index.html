Press Releases

Press Release Services

A release that is ready to run, and a genuine effort to place it in the right publication.

Many press release services end at the wire. The release is posted to a distribution site, and the process stops there. Reporters are busy, and an announcement from an unfamiliar source rarely gets a second look. My approach is to write a release that an editor or reporter would be proud to copy and paste, then follow up with courtesy and professionalism.

What is included

The release

A clear, accurate release in standard news format and AP style, with a headline, a lead that gets attention, quotes that sound like a person, and credible details that are easy to verify. Research is pulled from online sources, an interview, bullet points in an email—whatever method is preferred by the client. The client supplies the facts once, in whatever form is easiest, and receives a release that is ready to send.

A targeted media list

Local and trade reporters, editors and outlets that cover the relevant subject, hand-selected and personally vetted instead of pulled from a bulk database. The spreadsheet belongs to the client, and the list remains useful for every future announcement.

Personal pitches

Short, specific emails to the reporters who cover the industry segment, sent from a real address and followed up on by a real person. The client does not have to chase reporters.

A coverage report

A summary of the publications and individuals who received the release, who responded and where it appeared, so the results are clear. The client sees exactly who was contacted and the subsequent results, whether or not the release was published.

Wire distribution (optional)

Available as an add-on for announcements that benefit from broad syndication.

How it works

  1. A call of about 15 minutes covers the news, the audience and the intended outcome.
  2. I write the release and send it for review.
  3. Once the release is approved, I build the media list and send the pitches.
  4. I follow up and deliver the report.

Turnaround is usually 2 to 4 business days, with four rounds of revisions included.

Why a press release?

A law firm announcing a hire, a win or a new practice area may benefit from a press release. Real estate agents and brokerages use press releases to mark a record sale, a milestone or a new listing.

Charity fundraisers can be particularly effective, as many editors like to publish feel-good stories. Product launches, new locations, charity sponsorships and client appreciation events are also valid reasons for creating a press release. The service can benefit any organization with news and little time.

For an example of charity coverage, see the Love is Bald charity spotlight on The Pinnacle and First Tee Brings Character to the Fore on frittscausby.com.

Press releases are a valuable form of engagement, and in many cases a published release functions as an advertisement that reaches a more informed audience than a paid ad does. A press release also typically costs less than a single business-card-size print ad in a local or trade publication, where rates often start near $100 per insertion, and the release says far more about the business.

Questions

Is coverage guaranteed?

No. Editors decide what runs, and coverage cannot be promised. What is guaranteed is a professional release, targeted pitching and a report that covers what happened. A well-written release placed in front of the right reporters gives the news a better chance.

Is wire distribution available?

Yes, as an add-on.
