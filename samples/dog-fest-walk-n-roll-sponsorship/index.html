Real Estate Agent to Sponsor Dog Fest Walk ’n’ Roll

To help Canine Companions for Independence continue to provide highly-trained assistance dogs for children and adults with disabilities, a San Diego real estate agent is sponsoring the charity’s signature national fundraiser. Known as the Dog Fest Walk ’n’ Roll, this year’s event will be held on November 22 at NTC Park in Liberty Station.

“We are delighted to be a part of this effort,” says the agent. “Anyone who has ever had a dog knows the special kind of joy they bring. Can you imagine the bond they share with those who need them for doing things we take for granted every day, like turning on a light or opening a door? I encourage all to join us in supporting this great cause in any way they can.”

The agent and his wife were inspired when they met Norway, a yellow Labrador puppy that was being raised by a colleague at the brokerage. “She gives so much of her heart to these puppies for 18 to 20 months, then she turns them over to those in need,” explains his wife. “We had just lost our yellow lab when we met Norway and fell in love. Sponsoring this event is a great way to bring more dogs to those in need while honoring her phenomenal generosity.”

Canine Companions for Independence is a nonprofit that was founded in 1975 with the simple idea that the most advanced technology capable of transforming the lives of people with disabilities has a cold nose and a warm heart. There are no fees required to participate in the Dog Fest Walk ’n’ Roll. More information is available at www.CCI.org.

A 35-year veteran of the real estate industry, the agent is an avid supporter of numerous charitable organizations.
