Conference content for associations and event organizers

Conference summaries turned into articles, newsletters and posts can help professional associations, trade groups, chambers of commerce and event organizers. The service suits organizations that hold annual meetings, speaker series or member conferences and want content that keeps members engaged between events.

News-worthy events

  • Annual conferences and member meetings
  • Speaker series
  • Awards dinners

An annual conference supplies months of post-event articles, and a series from one speaker could provide a steady flow of shorter pieces. Awards dinners recognize members and give local outlets a ready-made story. Community outreach through volunteer days, scholarships and partnerships can show the value of the organization beyond its membership. New member services and benefits are also worth announcing, both to current members and to prospects.

What is included

In-person attendance at the event, recording of sessions with permission, and a package of articles, recaps, newsletter pieces and social posts, each checked against the recording and edited up to four times for accuracy. Press releases for awards, outreach programs and new member services are available as well. Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

Recording laws vary by state. Federal law and most states allow recording with the consent of one party, but about a dozen states, including California, Florida, Illinois and Maryland, generally require the consent of everyone in a private conversation, so speakers and panelists sign a release and attendees are notified. Presentation slides and handouts may belong to the speaker and are quoted or reproduced only with permission. Associations of competitors also follow antitrust guidelines at meetings, so recaps avoid reporting discussions of pricing or other competitively sensitive details. Continuing education claims are confirmed with the accrediting body. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.
