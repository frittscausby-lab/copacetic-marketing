Real Estate Agent Bikes 600 Miles for Challenged Athletes

A San Diego real estate agent recently participated in the Million Dollar Challenge, a 600-plus mile bike ride in support of the Challenged Athletes Foundation (CAF). “He does a lot more than talk about giving back, he takes action by donating his time, effort and resources,” said the manager of his office.

To take part in the weeklong event, which runs from San Francisco to San Diego, each participant must raise $10,000 for the CAF. This was the third consecutive year that the agent accepted the challenge, sometimes riding up to 119 miles a day over demanding California coastal terrain.

“It is so humbling to ride with these athletes,” said the agent, who participated with a paralyzed rider that used a hand cycle. “They are a true example of how to push beyond your limits.”

He was joined by Hall of Famer Bill Walton, who was the top fundraiser for the event with a total of $146,000. Also riding on a custom made bike was a Marine Corps Reservist who was paralyzed from the chest down at the age of 21. As a part of Operation Rebound, he participates in events like the Million Dollar Challenge.

“He is such an inspiration to all of us,” said the agent. “The beauty of this ride is that everyone works together to be the best we can be.”

This year’s challenge raised over $1.1 million for the CAF, which is dedicated to help people with physical disabilities pursue active lifestyles through physical fitness and competitive athletics.

The agent also recently participated in the CAF Triathlon Challenge, an event that raised over $1.4 million for the charity. He assisted a CAF athlete during a one-mile swim in La Jolla Cove.

“This foundation is so dear to my heart,” he added. “It isn’t about how far I can swim or bike, but how many people I can help stay active.”

About the Challenged Athletes Foundation

The Challenged Athletes Foundation is dedicated to help individuals with challenges overcome the barriers that keep them from achieving their dreams. By providing opportunities and support, the CAF helps people with physical disabilities pursue active lifestyles and find success in life.
