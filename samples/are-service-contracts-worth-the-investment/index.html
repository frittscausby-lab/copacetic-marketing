Are Service Contracts Worth the Investment?

If you are like me and subscribe to the wisdom of Ben Franklin, who famously uttered in 1736 that, “An ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure,” it’s likely you will agree that purchasing a service contract is a wise investment. Of course, keeping the system from breaking in the first place is better than having to order parts and wait for them to arrive, all while experiencing the onslaught of summer humidity that is iconic to the South.

It is worth pointing out that the average repair cost for an HVAC system ranges between $150 and $650, with the labor alone for technicians averaging between $75 and $150 per hour. With that in mind, it’s easy to see why many home owners view service contracts as an effective strategy for reducing costs.

The numbers referenced above are just a baseline scenario. In the heat of the summer or in the cold of the winter, if something happens to the system over the weekend and you want it repaired, the visit from the technician will carry along with it an emergency service call fee.

The cost of joining a maintenance plan would more than likely pay for itself after just one emergency or holiday visit when a repair was necessary. A few of the maintenance and prevention measures that are covered in a maintenance plan visit are listed below:

  • Inspect the system and ductwork
  • Check exposed equipment and pipes for leaks
  • Inspect thermostat condition and operation
  • Check heat strips, defrost controls and reversing valve of the heat pump
  • Start the capacity compressor and compressor fans to check recommended and actual MFD ratings
  • Inspect the indoor and outdoor coils
  • Add algae tabs in the drain pan
  • Test compressor protection device
  • Inspect condenser and overflow switch
  • Check current draw of compressor and the refrigerant operating pressure

There are also many intangibles that could result from purchasing a service contract. Small problems that may not seem like major issues to the average person can be easily identified by a licensed and trained technician. In many cases, small problems can be remedied before a disastrous event. A few of the other positive outcomes that could result from having regularly scheduled maintenance include:

  • Increased energy efficiency
  • Higher levels of comfort
  • Extend life of system
  • Reduce energy bills
  • Ensure safe operations

Many of our clients enjoy the benefits of our preventative maintenance plan. Joining can help save money on repairs and head off major issues, as members receive two maintenance visits per year, a 15 percent discount off the cost of a repair, a five percent discount on new equipment and duct systems, and no additional after-hours, holiday or weekend charges.

There are many other items included in a standard maintenance visit for maintenance plan members, and there are many intangible benefits that could potentially arise from having an expert visit your home and inspect your system for issues.
