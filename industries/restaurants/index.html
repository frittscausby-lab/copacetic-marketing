Industries

Food writing and press releases for restaurants, wineries and breweries

Press releases, chef profiles, menu descriptions and website copy help restaurants, bakeries, wineries and breweries fill tables and tasting rooms. The writing draws on years of food and dining features for Wrightsville Beach Magazine, including interviews with chefs, bakers and restaurant owners.

News-worthy events

  • Openings and renovations
  • New chefs and menus
  • Seasonal dishes and special events
  • Wine dinners, tastings and new releases
  • Awards and reviews
  • Charity events and community partnerships

A grand opening, a renovation or a new chef gives local editors a reason to visit. Seasonal menus, wine dinners, tastings and new releases fill an events calendar and keep regulars coming back. Awards and good reviews add credibility, while charity events and partnerships with local farms show the role the business plays in the community.

What is included

Available services include press releases, chef and owner profiles, menu descriptions, website copy, newsletters and blog posts with recipes. Information can be obtained from a short interview, a tasting and online research. Up to four edits are provided.

Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

Alcohol advertising follows federal rules from the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau and the rules of each state, and some states restrict drink promotions such as happy hour pricing. Claims such as “local,” “organic” or “farm-to-table” should be accurate, and allergen and dietary information is confirmed with the kitchen before publication. Contests and giveaways follow sweepstakes rules, and guests appear in photos only with their permission. Each business should confirm the rules of the state in which it operates.
