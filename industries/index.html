Industries

Industries

Every industry needs content development and distribution. The pages cover the events that earn coverage, the services that fit and the rules that apply.

Real Estate

Press releases, listing descriptions, property narratives and web pages deliver a competitive advantage for agents, teams, brokerages and developers. The writing draws on a background in real estate that includes creating press releases and marketing copy for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties since 2008.

Law Firms

Long-form content, client alerts, web pages and newsletter articles raise awareness for law firms and attorneys, demonstrating expertise and keeping clients informed. Each piece is written from a short interview and reviewed by the attorney before publication.

Medical and Dental Practices

Businesses that can benefit from copywriting and distribution services include physicians and dentists, group practices, hospitals and outpatient centers, physical therapy clinics, and medical spas/aesthetics practices.

Builders, Contractors and Developers

Builders, general contractors, developers, architects and engineering firms need press releases, project narratives, website copy and help with the written sections of proposals.

Nonprofits and Charity Events

Copywriting and distribution services help charities and nonprofits promote fundraisers and raise awareness. Press releases can generate valuable interest from community stakeholders. Charities and nonprofits also need to consistently apply for grants, which is why that is also offered as a service.

Financial Advisors

Copywriting and distribution services help financial advisors, wealth managers and planners who need help with LinkedIn posts, client newsletters and articles. The work suits independent registered investment advisers, advisors affiliated with a broker-dealer and small teams that want a steady, professional presence. Every piece is written from a short interview and approved by the firm before it is published.

Mortgage Brokers and Lenders

Blog posts, newsletters, sponsored articles and press releases help mortgage brokers, loan officers and lenders stay in front of borrowers, real estate agents and referral partners. The writing draws on years of sponsored content for a mortgage brokerage and a background in real estate sales.

Heating and Air Conditioning

Blog posts, newsletters and sponsored articles help heating and air conditioning companies explain their services to homeowners and stay top of mind between service calls. The writing turns technical topics, such as zoning, humidity and indoor air quality, into articles that homeowners actually read.

Associations and Event Organizers

Conference summaries turned into articles, newsletters and posts can help professional associations, trade groups, chambers of commerce and event organizers. The service suits organizations that hold annual meetings, speaker series or member conferences and want content that keeps members engaged between events.

Resorts, Clubs and Marinas

Press releases, articles and features raise awareness for resorts, hotels, golf and country clubs, marinas and yacht clubs. Support is also available for marketing collateral, including brochures, blog posts, website pages and newsletters for members and guests.

Restaurants, Wineries and Breweries

Press releases, chef profiles, menu descriptions and website copy help restaurants, bakeries, wineries and breweries fill tables and tasting rooms. The writing draws on years of food and dining features for Wrightsville Beach Magazine, including interviews with chefs, bakers and restaurant owners.

Retirement Communities

Press releases, sponsored articles, newsletters and website content are an essential component of a marketing plan for independent living, assisted living and continuing care retirement communities.
