The Importance of Real Estate Articles in Marketing

Fritts Causby  ·  Copacetic Marketing  ·  2 min read

A real estate article positions the agent as an expert, building trust long before there is a reason to buy or sell. Most people spend months thinking about a move before they contact anyone, and during that period they read. The agent or brokerage whose explanations they keep finding becomes the one they call.

The subjects that earn that trust are usually practical. A plain account of local market conditions with inventory, pricing figures and analyses from industry experts is more useful than an ad or a slogan. A guide to the steps between an accepted offer and the closing becomes a useful resource for buyers. A neighborhood piece that explains school boundaries, commute times and what a budget actually buys tells a relocating family more than a dozen listings.

Market conditions set the agenda. When the data show first-time buyers returning to a region, articles on financing options and inspection basics become essential to the brokerage and its agents. Similarly, an article that outlines how to negotiate is timely when inventory tightens. The articles that perform the best answer questions people are actually asking.

Articles carry weight with search engines, but the mechanism is simpler than many people think. Search engines reward pages that answer specific questions clearly. An article explaining how property taxes are reassessed after a sale in a particular county will draw search traffic for years, long after a social media post on the same subject has disappeared from the feed. This is the holy grail that is often referred to as evergreen content.

A single well-made article can also feed countless other channels. It can be condensed into a newsletter, divided into a week of social posts, sent to a client for reference, or pitched to a local business journal as the basis for an article. The research is done once.

For a sample of a home feature written for a local magazine, see Every Detail Tells a Story on frittscausby.com. For an article that compares real estate with other investments, see Stocks vs. Real Estate on The Pinnacle.

Content falls short for a variety of reasons. Some articles may go into too much detail about the agent instead of engaging the reader. Others may just reproduce what every other brokerage is already saying or read as generic marketing copy that was generated for the sole purpose of filling a calendar. A reader knows within a paragraph and so do the search engines.

As it is with many other things in life, the key is delivering value. Through market updates, process guides and neighborhood articles, I help agents and brokerages become an essential resource for current and prospective clients. Reach out if you’re ready to start building a library of content that shows the value and depth of the insights you offer.
