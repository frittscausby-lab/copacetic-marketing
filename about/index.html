About Fritts Causby

A writer and PR consultant with a background in luxury real estate, legal content and finance.

I have written for a living since 2008, and real estate has been my focus for most of that time. As a contractor for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties, I have been fortunate to support agents nationwide. Most of my work has been remote, and I have worked with clients across the country. The pitches I have created for the company have led to coverage in Robb Report and Mansion Global. That work taught me how to make a property or a person memorable in a few hundred words and how to write for very busy readers.

Learning the nuances of different markets has its challenges. I develop area-specific content through research and the expertise of my clients, and my role has often been to simply refine what they already know.

Over the years, I added services. I developed sponsored content for the Wilmington Business Journal for eight years, and I have produced legal content for Ward and Smith, P.A., a regional law firm with offices across the Carolinas, since 2021. My work has also been published in Inman Real Estate News.

As a writer for Wrightsville Beach Magazine, I have covered a wide array of subject matter, including lifestyle, art, culture, travel and, of course, real estate. A small sample of that work is available at frittscausby.com.

Personal interest led me to build The Pinnacle, an online publication covering luxury real estate, financial markets and the aspirational lifestyle.

Other clients have included Coldwell Banker Sea Coast Advantage, Pacific Sotheby’s International Realty, Piatt Sotheby’s International Realty, Market Consulting Mortgage and hundreds of individual agents nationwide.

I work with each client directly, from the first call to the final draft. Over time, I hope the firm will create opportunities for new writers, graphic designers and young professionals.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in English from North Carolina State University and a master’s degree in Human Resources and Organization Development from National University. I live on the North Carolina coast, and outside of work I surf, play golf and travel when I can.

Thank you for reading, Fritts

A note on samples

Most of the content I have produced was published under the names of my clients. I do not post or share work that I was paid to create as a ghostwriter, out of loyalty to them and to protect their search presence and brand reputation. This has always been important to me from an ethical standpoint, and the same discretion applies to new clients. To get a feel for the writing, see the samples on this site, frittscausby.com or thepinnacle.ink.
