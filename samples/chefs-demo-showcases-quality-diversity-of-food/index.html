Chef’s Demo Showcases Quality, Diversity of Food

When I point out that having a chance to sample a variety of fine cuisine is one of the best parts about working here, it is not a surprise for many people. For those who are unfamiliar with our community, the assumption is that residents and staff have access to a cafeteria, not a restaurant.

This could not be farther from the truth, however, and luckily for me, dispelling the notion is as easy as…avoiding an obvious pun. It is as simple as inviting potential residents to experience firsthand the incomparable culinary talents of our executive chef and pastry chef, which is exactly what we did recently during an exclusive, four-course demonstration.

Were the menu available to me at the time of this writing, it would certainly make for a high-quality ingredient for use in the article. That is not the case, and the fact the menu is not currently available is indicative of the exceptional quality standards in place at the restaurant, where the emphasis is on creating a farm-to-table experience and many of the menu items are constantly changing.

To put it simply, the menu here was designed to be as fresh and vibrant as our residents. Our residents are what make our community great. They bring a variety of life experiences, tastes and preferences to the table, so it just made sense that our menu would need to be as diverse as they are.

Incorporating locally-sourced ingredients of the highest possible quality, the menu here is a tasty fusion of a variety of different styles. There are four new specials every day and the menu changes every week based on the preferences of our residents and what is currently available from the local markets.

The restaurant offers Certified Angus Beef and a new Catch-of-the-Day, every day. The menu is dynamic and fluid but the favorite mainstays that many are accustomed to seeing are always available. This makes our menu an effective blend of the adventurous and the tried-and-true.

Chef Mark is far from the stereotype of the angry chef or Soup Nazi. In fact, he is patient and humble, willing to listen to feedback or seek out the preferences of our residents. Chef Mark understands that many of our residents are not Southerners, and that the ones who are from the South are always willing to try new things. This is why he characterizes his kitchen as regional, with a twist—something that is never boring and truly unlimited in its appeal.

One of the coolest things about Chef Mark is that he invites the residents to bring in their favorite recipes, so that he and the staff can prepare them for everyone. The preparation and enjoyment of food is a common, universal thread. It brings people together in ways that are impossible to describe.

Being able to share a family favorite that has been carefully refined, tested and proven over the generations is something that our residents love. Many of our residents would like to have everyone who lives here over for dinner sometime, but the cost and the clean-up prevents that from happening. Having Chef Mark prepare and serve a traditional family recipe is similar to having everyone over for dinner, with none of the downside.

It is also worth noting that our residents are encouraged to share recipes and favorite foods from all over the world. Many of our residents are extremely well-traveled, and it has become a challenge, hobby and a source of pride for them to find something new, exciting and adventurous for the chef to create.

Of course, this creates a plethora of opportunities for social engagement, a veritable smorgasbord of social interaction, something that is well known to enrich people’s lives and foster a sense of community. We believe that our restaurant sets us apart from other Life Plan Communities, and we invite you to take a private tour or sign up for our next Chef’s Demo and experience the difference.
