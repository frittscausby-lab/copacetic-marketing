Conference to Content

Turn the effort behind a conference, panel or client event into content that keeps working for months.

A conference is expensive to produce and takes months to plan. When the event ends, the insights and conversations that justified the investment usually leave the room with the audience. Conference to Content is a service that captures the substance of an event in real time and converts it into articles, posts and summary pieces the firm can use for the next several months.

How it works

  1. I attend the event in person. Panels, keynotes, breakout sessions and informal remarks are recorded with the permission of each speaker, and I take notes alongside the recording so that names, figures and quotes are captured accurately.
  2. I draft articles, LinkedIn posts, summary emails and recap pages from the recordings, each piece written in the voice of the attributed presenter and reviewed before publication.
  3. I deliver the finished content on an agreed schedule, which is usually weekly over the four to eight weeks following the event.

What the client receives

A single two-day conference typically supports a package of four to eight articles, a dozen or more social posts and a summary email for attendees and prospects. Every name, figure and quote is checked against the recording before publication, so an article that pulls a line from a panelist can be trusted to match what the panelist actually said.

Why it works

Content produced from a conference carries two advantages that content produced from a brief does not. The ideas are already vetted by people the audience respects, and the writing has the specificity that only comes from the room. A reader who missed the event sees what mattered, and a reader who attended sees the firm doing something useful with the material.

Who it suits

Law firms with client-facing seminars, legal CLEs and bar conferences. Real estate brokerages with annual company meetings, agent training weeks and industry events. Business associations and chambers of commerce with speaker series and member conferences. Any organization whose calendar includes events worth attending.

Experience

I have attended legal conferences and client seminars for a regional law firm with offices across the Carolinas since 2021, and produced articles, posts and summary pieces from recordings of panels, keynotes and roundtables. Travel is handled at cost and discussed in advance.

Pricing

Scope depends on the length of the event, the number of pieces and the publication schedule, so each event is quoted individually.
