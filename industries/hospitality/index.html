Press releases and features for resorts, clubs and marinas

Press releases, articles and features raise awareness for resorts, hotels, golf and country clubs, marinas and yacht clubs. Support is also available for marketing collateral, including brochures, blog posts, website pages and newsletters for members and guests.

News-worthy events

  • Renovations and reopenings
  • New chefs or menus
  • Tournaments and events
  • Awards and rankings

A renovation or a reopening invites travel and lifestyle editors to take a fresh look at a property. A new chef or a seasonal menu gives writers a reason to visit. Tournaments, regattas and holiday events draw local coverage and fill a calendar, while awards and rankings provide third-party credibility. New amenities, partnerships with local businesses, and charity events hosted at the property round out the year.

What is included

A press release or a feature written around the property and the occasion, with research, interviews with management, the chef or the club professional, and up to four edits. Outreach to travel, lifestyle and local editors is available as an add-on, along with brochures, blog posts, website pages and newsletters. Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

Since May 2025, the FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees has required hotels and other short-term lodging businesses that advertise a price to show the total price, including mandatory fees, more prominently than other pricing, so a rate in a release should be the full rate. Alcohol promotions follow federal and state advertising rules, and contests and giveaways follow sweepstakes rules. Accessibility claims and award names are confirmed before publication, and guests and members appear in photos only with their permission. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.
