Monthly content

A ready-to-send article every month

For loan officers and real estate agents.

Most loan officers and real estate agents know they should stay in touch with past clients and prospects, but few have time to write a newsletter every month. The monthly content service delivers one finished article each month, written for borrowers and ready to send under the name of the subscriber.

What is included

  • One article of 600 to 800 words each month, on topics such as credit, refinancing, buying a first home, renovation loans and the housing market
  • A short version for LinkedIn and Facebook, and a subject line for email
  • The name, company and contact details of the subscriber added to each article
  • Delivery in the first week of the month as a Word file and as plain text, ready to paste into Mailchimp, Constant Contact or any newsletter platform

One subscriber per city

Each article is exclusive to one loan officer and one real estate agent in a city, so two competitors never send the same piece to the same neighborhood. Cities are reserved in the order subscribers sign up.

Compliance

Articles avoid rate promises and guarantees, and rates and figures are current as of the month of delivery. Each subscriber adds the disclosures that apply, such as an NMLS number or brokerage name, and reviews the article against the policies of the company before sending it. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.
