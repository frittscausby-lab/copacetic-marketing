What Impact Can Charitable Initiatives Have on Real Estate Development and Community Engagement?

Fritts Causby  ·  Copacetic Marketing  ·  2 min read

A real estate development changes the landscape of a community dramatically, and a developer that does not consider the potential negative impacts to the neighborhood is certain to face a significant amount of backlash from the residents. Adding a charitable initiative to a project can reduce this backlash, further the interests of the developer and help the community, creating a win-win for everyone involved.

There are a variety of methods for achieving this objective. A developer could donate to a local school, establish a long-term partnership with a housing nonprofit, build a trail system, set aside open space, or make ongoing contributions to community organizations. The options are only limited to the imagination of the people involved.

Residents who oppose a project usually oppose what they expect to lose: parking, green space, a view, the character of a street. A development that commits to a public park, a library branch or a community room gives neighbors something concrete to weigh against those losses, and it gives the planning board a record of enforceable commitments. Town hall meetings can assist in the approval process, as residents value having a chance to review the plans and ask for changes.

Buyers, community stakeholders and tenants increasingly consider the actions, or inactions, of a developer and/or brokerage beyond the transaction. Community features that are built and maintained instead of just promised can become a part of the sales presentation. The residents who use them serve as a credible advocate for the developer and the project.

Housing affordability is another critical issue. Partnerships with nonprofits focused on affordable housing allow a developer to include a range of price points in a project. Mixed-income developments are more complex to finance and manage, however, these projects answer the affordability question that now follows nearly every new project through the approval process.

At the brokerage level, charitable involvement creates a positive touch point with the local community. An office that sponsors a fundraiser, a team that volunteers with a local charity or an agent who donates a portion of each commission builds the kind of local standing that produces referrals.

Communicating these efforts is a separate matter, and it is where many well-intended programs fall short. A charitable initiative described in the language of a marketing campaign reads as a marketing campaign. A press release about a fundraiser should name the organization, state what was raised or built, credit the people who did the work and stop.

For examples, see the Love is Bald charity spotlight on The Pinnacle and First Tee Brings Character to the Fore on frittscausby.com.
