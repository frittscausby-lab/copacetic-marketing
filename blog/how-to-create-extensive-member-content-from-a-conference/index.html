How to Create Extensive Member Content from a Conference

Fritts Causby  ·  Copacetic Marketing  ·  2 min read

An annual conference is often the largest investment an association makes in its members; however, the expertise that is shared during the event is rarely used again. With planning, a single conference can deliver a steady flow of member content.

The planning starts before the event. Speakers agree in writing to being recorded and quoted. Each session could be reproduced for a variety of purposes, such as a feature article, a newsletter or a series of social posts.

During the event, every session is recorded and notes capture the moments a recording misses, such as audience questions, comments and reactions. Photos of speakers and attendees could give each piece a visual anchor and are only taken with permission.

After the event, the work is paced. A summary for members is distributed within a week, while interest is high. Feature articles built around a single speaker or theme could follow based on the preference of the client. Shorter pieces fill the newsletter between features, and social posts point back to the full articles on the website of the association.

Accuracy matters more than speed. Every quote and figure is checked against the recording, and speakers review the pieces that feature them. Recaps leave out any discussion of pricing or other competitively sensitive topics.

The result is a library of content that reflects the expertise of the members, published on a schedule that keeps them engaged until the next conference. Copacetic Marketing attends, records and writes the full package.
