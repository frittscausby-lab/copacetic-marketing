Press releases and content for retirement communities

Press releases, sponsored articles, newsletters and website content are an essential component of a marketing plan for independent living, assisted living and continuing care retirement communities.

News-worthy events

New buildings, expansions and renovations are the largest announcements. Stories from the residents show life in the community better than any brochure, and many enjoy being interviewed. Milestone birthdays and anniversaries are also favorites of local editors.

Wellness programs, dining and new amenities, community partnerships, volunteer work by residents and awards for care or service all give readers a reason to look closer. New executive directors and medical directors are worth announcing as well.

What is included

The service could include a press release or a sponsored article written around the community and the occasion, with research, interviews with staff and residents, and up to four edits. Outreach to local editors is available as an add-on, along with resident newsletters, website pages and brochure copy.

Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

Residents appear in stories and photos only with their written consent, and health details are left out unless the resident asks to share them. Communities that provide health care may also have HIPAA obligations. Claims about levels of care, licensing and pricing must match the licenses and contracts of the community, and the Fair Housing Act limits advertising that suggests a preference based on age or other protected characteristics outside the senior housing exemptions in the law. Each community should confirm its obligations. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.
