Ghostwriting for financial advisors

Copywriting and distribution services help financial advisors, wealth managers and planners who need help with LinkedIn posts, client newsletters and articles. The work suits independent registered investment advisers, advisors affiliated with a broker-dealer and small teams that want a steady, professional presence. Every piece is written from a short interview and approved by the firm before it is published.

News-worthy events

  • Market commentary and client newsletters
  • LinkedIn posts on common client questions
  • New advisors, office openings and designations

Client newsletters can cover sector themes, notable stock and fund ideas presented as the opinion of the advisor, and macroeconomic conditions such as interest rates, inflation and employment data. LinkedIn posts work best when the posts answer frequently asked questions. A new advisor, an office opening or a new professional designation is a valid reason for creating a press release.

What is included

A newsletter, article or series of LinkedIn posts written from a short interview, with market research and up to four edits to ensure accuracy. Drafts are delivered in a form that is easy for a compliance team to review and archive. Press releases for new advisors, offices and designations are available as well. Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

Registered investment advisers follow the SEC Marketing Rule, which governs testimonials, endorsements and performance in advertising. Broker-dealers follow FINRA Rule 2210, which has long required a qualified principal to approve retail communications before use; FINRA proposed changes to that requirement in 2026, so the current procedures of each firm control. Both sets of rules require communications to be fair and balanced, and both require records to be kept. Content avoids guarantees and performance promises, and every piece goes to the compliance team of the firm before publishing. Nothing on this page constitutes legal or compliance advice.
