Services

The services presented here are not a complete list. There is no such thing as a one-size-fits-all solution, so the process is fluid and the work is adapted to the needs of each client.

Press releases and media outreach

An event, a new hire, a service expansion or a fundraiser is an opportunity to seek coverage with a professionally written press release. The service includes a targeted list of local and trade reporters, personal pitch emails and a report on the coverage that follows. The client approves one document and I handle the list, pitches and follow-up.

Press release services

Ghostwriting for attorneys

Blog posts, client alerts and bylined articles with insights and guidance from the perspective of the attorney, written from a short interview and reviewed by the attorney before publishing. Each piece is customized to the voice of the firm and the attorney. The content draws from my experience writing legal content since 2021. The attorney spends about 30 minutes in conversation and reviews the article before it is published.

Ghostwriting services

Ghostwriting for real estate professionals

Market updates, newsletters, agent bios, LinkedIn posts and area guides built on the insights and knowledge of the agent, leveraging the experience of a writer who has worked in real estate since 2005 in sales, marketing, administration and copywriting. Clients and prospects hear from the agent on a regular schedule, even in the busiest months.

Ghostwriting services

Executive and LinkedIn ghostwriting

Ongoing posts and articles for owners and subject matter experts who want a steady presence but do not have the time to write every post. Nothing is posted without the approval of the client. An optional add-on covers posting and engagement support, with each reply drafted for the approval of the client before posting.

Newsletter distribution and management

A newsletter is a cost-effective means of connecting with customers. This service covers the writing, formatting, scheduling and distribution of newsletters on a regular cadence, with list management and basic reporting, on the platform the client prefers, including Mailchimp, Constant Contact and Beehiiv. The client approves each issue, and the writing, formatting and sending are handled.

Conference to Content

The effort and planning that go into a conference are significant, so why should the content evaporate when the conference ends? I attend the client event, panel or conference in person, record the sessions for accuracy and write the articles, posts and summary pieces. This is evergreen content that keeps working long after the event is over. A single conference can supply months of articles and posts, and every name, figure and quote is checked against the recording before publication.

Conference to Content

Architectural property narratives and listing descriptions

Architectural narratives, listing descriptions, property stories and community lifestyle pages for neighborhoods and developments. Many houses have a resort-like backyard and ocean views. The story behind a home is often what earns coverage from media outlets. A home with a story becomes a resume builder for the agent, and a written narrative that honors the architecture keeps the agent's name tied to the property long after it sells. Unique property websites

A dedicated website for each listing can set an agent apart. It shows sellers the effort behind the marketing and can build positive word of mouth. Each site includes the brokerage, license and fair housing information that advertising rules require. Sellers immediately recognize the value, time and effort involved with building a website that is specifically for their home.

Video scripts

Scripts for property tours, neighborhood features, brand videos and social clips, tailored to the platform and length the client chooses. A finished script shortens the shoot and keeps the message on point.

Sponsored content

Sponsored articles that describe the value and services of a business and read like journalism, written to the requirements of the publication where the client has purchased space. A business that has paid for the space deserves an article that readers will finish.

Reputation management

Calm, clear messaging when something goes wrong, and ongoing attention to how a business is described online. A prepared statement is ready before the questions arrive.

Website creation and management

I design and build focused, straightforward websites for small businesses and professionals: a few clearly written pages that load quickly, explain the business and make contact easy. I also work with WordPress and Wix where a client already uses one of them. Three current examples are frittscausby.com, thepinnacle.ink and this site. The writing is included, and each site can carry a news section that collects the press releases and articles produced together.
