Writing for real estate agents, teams and brokerages

Press releases, listing descriptions, property narratives and web pages deliver a competitive advantage for agents, teams, brokerages and developers. The writing draws on a background in real estate that includes creating press releases and marketing copy for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties since 2008.

News-worthy events

  • Notable sales
  • Luxury and architecturally significant listings
  • New agents, teams and offices
  • Awards and production rankings
  • Market reports
  • Community involvement and sponsorships

A record sale or a significant listing earns coverage beyond the local market, and a well-told property narrative gives editors a reason to feature the home. New agents, teams and offices are worth announcing to the local business community; awards and production rankings add credibility. Market reports and newsletters keep an agent in front of past clients between transactions, while community involvement shows the agent is invested in the area.

What is included

A listing description, property story or press release written around the home and the occasion, with research, an interview with the agent and up to four edits. Agent bios, newsletters, market updates, LinkedIn posts and area guides are available as well. Pitching to luxury and national publications is available as an add-on service.

Larger projects can grow into omnichannel campaigns, with one story carried through press coverage, email newsletters, social media and the website. AI tools help with research and measuring results, and every word is written and reviewed by a person.

Rules that apply

The Fair Housing Act prohibits advertising that indicates a preference or limitation based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status or national origin, so copy describes the property rather than the ideal buyer. State license laws also apply. In North Carolina, for example, a broker needs the consent of the broker-in-charge before advertising, and every advertisement, including a social media post, must include the name of the firm. Many states have similar rules. Square footage, school information and sale prices are confirmed before publication, and a sale price is announced only after closing and with the consent of the parties. Photos are used only with the rights to publish them. Each brokerage should confirm the rules of the state in which it operates. Nothing on this page constitutes legal advice.
